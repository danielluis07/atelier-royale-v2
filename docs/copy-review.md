## Editorial direction

The voice is calm, practical and familiar. Product descriptions explain how a
piece fits, feels or works using the catalog's existing facts. The lookbook keeps
its setting and seasonal atmosphere, with fewer dramatic fragments. Interface
messages use the wording a Brazilian shopper would expect.

## Storefront and demo messages

Color names, material composition, finishes, sorting options and accessible image
descriptions now use Portuguese. Familiar trade terms such as selvedge, moleskin
and Goodyear remain. Piece and color IDs keep their existing values, including
image keys and saved cart references. Color filters accept the Portuguese names
and existing English names or IDs.

The catalog still uses American clothing and footwear sizes, measurements in
inches, US delivery destinations and BRL prices. The copy identifies these where
they affect a choice. Standard shipping is consistently free **from R$ 250**,
matching the checkout calculation, including at exactly R$ 250.

Checkout says “Esta compra é uma simulação. Nenhum valor será cobrado.” Its action
is “Simular pedido”. Confirmation uses “Total simulado” and explains that no
charge or email occurs. The delivery steps describe what would happen in a real
order. Newsletter success says “Inscrição simulada. Este site de demonstração não
envia e-mails.”

The Brazilian Portuguese writing conventions are recorded in DESIGN.md §8.
