# Catalog models D, E and F

The owner selected these three new men for the Shirts on-body galleries in
issue #38. Their identity references were generated for batch 4b and are
recorded in [the cast prompts](shirts-cast-prompts.json). Reuse these references
for later photographs of the same men.

- **Model D — Viking appearance:** long reddish-brown hair, a full thick beard,
  broad weathered face and a large sturdy build. Modern workwear styling.
- **Model E — Very short hair and moustache:** very short dark buzz cut,
  distinct moustache, otherwise clean-shaven cheeks and chin, narrower angular
  face and lean build. Keep the moustache separate from a beard.
- **Model F — Blond and clean-shaven:** blond hair, no beard or moustache,
  softer oval face and a medium build. Keep him clearly distinct from Model D.

Identity references: [Model D](cast-model-d.jpg), [Model E](cast-model-e.jpg),
and [Model F](cast-model-f.jpg). These are production references, not catalog
image keys, and live outside `public/images/`.

Use distinct faces, hair, facial hair and builds. Keep each man's identity
consistent through a Colourway's front, back and detail photographs. Save the
first selected photograph of each new man as that model's identity reference
before creating more views. Each Colourway's back and detail use its front
photograph as the garment and session reference.

All catalog photographs use the same blank seamless stone backdrop
(`#F3F1EC`). Every man stands still in a deliberate pose. Vary stance, body
angle and arm placement between models while keeping the garment visible.
Avoid scenery, props, costumes, logos, text and watermarks.
