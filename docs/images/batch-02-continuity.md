## Background and lighting

The owner's instruction during production supersedes the location background
rule in issues #11 and #35 for catalog imagery. Every still life and every
on-body front, back and detail photograph uses a blank seamless warm stone
background (`#F3F1EC`). No scenery, architecture, plants, location objects or
props appear behind models. Soft diffused studio daylight and the restrained
muted grade unify the catalog. Editorial imagery keeps its own direction.
